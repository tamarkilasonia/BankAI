# Domestic transfers

> MOCK policy of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Transfers between your own accounts

Transfers between your own BankAI accounts are free and instant, 24 hours a day. If the accounts are in different currencies, the bank's exchange rate at the time of the transfer is applied.

## Transfers to other BankAI customers

Transfers to other BankAI customers in GEL are free and arrive instantly. You need the recipient's account number (IBAN) and full name.

## Transfers to other banks in Georgia

Transfers in GEL to accounts at other Georgian banks cost 0.50 GEL each. Transfers sent before 16:00 on a business day arrive the same day; later transfers arrive on the next business day.

## Standing orders

A standing order pays a fixed amount on a fixed date, for example monthly rent. Standing orders are set up in the mobile app; each execution is charged like a normal transfer of the same type.

## Cancelling a transfer

Instant transfers cannot be cancelled once confirmed. A transfer to another bank can be cancelled in the app only while its status is "pending", usually until the 16:00 processing cut-off.
