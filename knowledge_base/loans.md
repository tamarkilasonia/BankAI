# Consumer and auto loans

> MOCK product terms of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Consumer Loan

The Consumer Loan is an unsecured personal loan. The annual interest rate is 14.50%. You can borrow from 500.00 GEL to 50,000.00 GEL with a term from 6 to 60 months. Repayment is in equal monthly instalments (annuity).

## Auto Loan

The Auto Loan finances a new or used car. The annual interest rate is 11.90%. You can borrow from 5,000.00 GEL to 150,000.00 GEL with a term from 12 to 84 months. The car serves as collateral and must have comprehensive insurance for the whole loan term.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be 21 to 65 years old, have a regular income for at least 6 months, and pass a credit history check. Monthly loan repayments, including existing loans, may not exceed 50% of verified monthly income.

## Applying for a loan

Loans can be requested in the mobile app or at a branch. You need an ID document and proof of income. A decision on a Consumer Loan is usually given within 1 business day; an Auto Loan within 3 business days.

## Early repayment

A Consumer Loan can be repaid early at any time without a fee. Early repayment of an Auto Loan within the first 12 months costs 1% of the repaid amount; after 12 months it is free.

## Missed payments

A missed instalment is charged a late fee of 0.1% of the overdue amount per day. Missed payments are reported to the credit bureau and can affect future credit decisions.
