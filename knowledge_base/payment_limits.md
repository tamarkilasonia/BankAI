# Payment limits

> MOCK policy of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Daily card limits

The default daily limit for card purchases is 10,000.00 GEL. The default daily limit for ATM cash withdrawals is 3,000.00 GEL. Gold Debit Card holders have a daily purchase limit of 20,000.00 GEL.

## Daily transfer limits

Transfers in the mobile app are limited to 20,000.00 GEL per day by default. After additional identity verification the limit can be raised to 50,000.00 GEL per day. International transfers are limited to the equivalent of 50,000.00 GEL per day.

## Changing your limits

Limits can be lowered at any time in the mobile app. Raising a limit requires confirmation with two-factor authentication, and a temporary increase can be set for up to 7 days. The assistant cannot change limits.
