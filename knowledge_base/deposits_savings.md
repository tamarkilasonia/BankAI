# Savings and deposits

> MOCK product terms of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Flexible Savings Account

The Flexible Savings Account pays an annual interest rate of 7.50% on GEL balances. Money can be withdrawn at any time without a fee. Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly. There is no monthly fee.

## 12-Month Term Deposit

The 12-Month Term Deposit pays an annual interest rate of 10.00%. The minimum amount is 1,000.00 GEL and the money is fixed for 12 months. Interest is paid at the end of the term.

## Early withdrawal from a term deposit

A term deposit can be closed early, but then interest is recalculated at the Flexible Savings Account rate of 7.50% for the time the money was deposited.

## Opening a savings account or deposit

Savings accounts and term deposits are opened in the mobile app in a few minutes. You need an existing BankAI current account.
