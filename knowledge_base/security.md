# Security

> MOCK policy of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## What the bank will never ask for

BankAI will never ask for your PIN, your full card number, your password or one-time codes (OTP) by phone, e-mail, SMS or chat. Anyone asking for them is attempting fraud. The assistant cannot see or reset passwords.

## Phishing and scams

Be careful with messages that create urgency, ask you to click a link, or ask you to move money to a "safe account". Always log in through the official mobile app, never through links in messages.

## Reporting suspicious activity

If you see a transaction you do not recognise, block the card in the mobile app and report the transaction through the app or the 24/7 hotline (MOCK number: +995 000 00 00 00). Card transactions can be disputed within 120 days of the transaction date.

## Two-factor authentication

Logging in and confirming payments in the mobile app requires two-factor authentication: something you know (PIN or password) and something you have (your registered phone). Large or unusual payments require an additional confirmation.

## Resetting your password

You can reset your online banking password in the mobile app with your ID number and a one-time code sent to your registered phone, or at a branch with an ID document.
