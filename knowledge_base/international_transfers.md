# International transfers

> MOCK policy of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Fees for international transfers

International transfers in USD or EUR are sent through the SWIFT network. The fee is 0.20% of the amount, with a minimum of 15.00 GEL and a maximum of 150.00 GEL per transfer. Correspondent and receiving banks may deduct their own charges, which BankAI does not control.

## Processing time

International transfers sent before 15:00 on a business day are processed the same day. They usually reach the recipient within 1 to 3 business days, depending on the destination country and the banks involved.

## Required details

To send money abroad you need the recipient's full name and address, their account number or IBAN, the recipient bank's SWIFT/BIC code, and the purpose of the payment.

## Currencies

International transfers can be sent in USD and EUR. If you send from a GEL account, the amount is converted at the bank's exchange rate at the moment of the transfer.

## Compliance checks

Every international transfer is screened against sanctions lists. Transfers may be delayed or rejected if information is missing or the payment cannot be completed lawfully; in that case the fee is refunded.
