# Mortgages

> MOCK product terms of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Standard Mortgage

The Standard Mortgage finances the purchase of a home. The annual interest rate is 9.50%. You can borrow from 20,000.00 GEL to 1,000,000.00 GEL with a term from 60 to 360 months.

## Down payment and loan-to-value

The minimum down payment is 20% of the property's value, so the bank lends at most 80% of the value (loan-to-value ratio of 80%). The property value is set by an independent valuation that costs 150.00 GEL.

## Insurance

The property must be insured against fire and natural disasters for the whole mortgage term, with the bank as beneficiary. Life insurance for the borrower is recommended but not required.

## Applying for a mortgage

Mortgage applications are made at a branch. You need an ID document, proof of income, and the property documents. A decision is usually given within 5 business days after the valuation.

## Early repayment of a mortgage

A mortgage can be repaid early. Within the first 36 months the early repayment fee is 1% of the repaid amount; after 36 months early repayment is free.
