# Cards

> MOCK policy of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Debit cards

BankAI offers two debit cards linked to a current account. The Classic Debit Card has no monthly fee. The Gold Debit Card costs 5.00 GEL per month and includes travel insurance and higher spending limits. Both cards work for contactless payments, online shopping and ATM withdrawals in Georgia and abroad.

## Credit card

The Standard Credit Card has a credit limit of up to 10,000.00 GEL, a monthly fee of 2.00 GEL and an annual interest rate of 24.00% on balances that are not repaid in time. Purchases have up to 55 interest-free days if the full statement balance is paid by the due date. Cash withdrawals with the credit card are charged interest from the day of the withdrawal.

## Ordering and activating a card

A card can be ordered in the mobile app or at any branch. Standard delivery to a branch takes 5 business days and is free; express courier delivery within 2 business days costs 20.00 GEL. A new card is activated by making the first payment with the PIN or by activating it in the mobile app.

## Lost or stolen card

If a card is lost or stolen, block it immediately in the mobile app (Cards > Block card) or call the 24/7 card hotline (MOCK number: +995 000 00 00 00). Blocking is free and takes effect immediately. A replacement card costs 10.00 GEL and arrives within 5 business days. Report any transactions you do not recognise so they can be disputed.

## PIN codes

The PIN is set in the mobile app when the card is activated and can be changed there at any time. The bank never asks for your PIN by phone, e-mail or chat. After three wrong PIN entries the card is temporarily blocked for 24 hours.
