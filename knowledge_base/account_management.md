# Account management

> MOCK policy of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Opening an account

Anyone aged 18 or older with a valid ID document can open a Current Account in the mobile app or at a branch. Opening an account is free and takes about 10 minutes.

## Closing an account

You can close an account in the mobile app or at a branch free of charge. The remaining balance is transferred to an account you choose, and cards linked to the account are cancelled. Accounts with an outstanding loan or overdraft cannot be closed until it is repaid.

## Changing personal details

Your phone number, e-mail and address can be updated in the mobile app. Changing your name requires visiting a branch with the supporting documents.

## Account statements

Statements for any period are available for free in the mobile app as PDF files. A printed statement from a branch costs 5.00 GEL.

## Frozen accounts

An account can be frozen at the customer's request, by a court order, or when the bank detects suspicious activity. While an account is frozen, outgoing payments are blocked; incoming payments are still accepted. To unfreeze an account, contact the bank through the app or a branch.
