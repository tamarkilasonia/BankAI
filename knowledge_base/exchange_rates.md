# Exchange rates

> MOCK policy of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## How exchange rates are set

BankAI publishes buy and sell rates for USD and EUR against GEL. Rates are updated every business day at 10:00 and may change during the day when markets move. The difference between the buy and sell rate is the bank's spread.

## Rates shown by the assistant

The assistant shows a single illustrative mid-market rate from a MOCK rate table. It is not a live market rate and is not the rate at which a real conversion would be executed.

## Converting money between accounts

Converting money between your own accounts in different currencies is free of additional fees; the bank's buy or sell rate is applied at the moment of conversion.

## Paying abroad with a card

When you pay with a card in a foreign currency, the card network's exchange rate is used and a 1.5% conversion fee is added.
