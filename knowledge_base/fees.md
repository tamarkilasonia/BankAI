# Fee schedule

> MOCK fee schedule of a fictional bank. Illustrative values only.

## Account fees

Opening and maintaining a Current Account is free: the monthly fee is 0.00 GEL. Online statements are free; a printed statement from a branch costs 5.00 GEL.

## Card fees

The Classic Debit Card has no monthly fee. The Gold Debit Card costs 5.00 GEL per month. The Standard Credit Card costs 2.00 GEL per month. A replacement card costs 10.00 GEL and express card delivery costs 20.00 GEL.

## ATM withdrawal fees

Cash withdrawals at BankAI ATMs are free. Withdrawals at other banks' ATMs in Georgia cost 0.20% of the amount, minimum 2.00 GEL. Withdrawals abroad cost 2% of the amount, minimum 5.00 GEL.

## Transfer fees

Transfers between your own accounts and to other BankAI customers are free. Transfers to other banks in Georgia cost 0.50 GEL. International transfers cost 0.20% of the amount, minimum 15.00 GEL and maximum 150.00 GEL.

## Currency conversion

Card payments in a currency other than the card's currency are converted at the card network rate plus a 1.5% conversion fee. Conversions between your own accounts use the bank's buy and sell rates, with no additional fee.
